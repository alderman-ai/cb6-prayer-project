Progress

The signature element. The bar is the wall: what has been given is solid gold stone, what remains is the perforated screen that fronts the new entrance — still open, still letting light through. One ink hairline marks the seam between built and unbuilt. No thermometer, no bulb, no red.

Two numbers to confirm before publication. The USD figures above use an assumed rate of 21 CZK to 1 USD (54M ≈ $2.6M, 14M ≈ $670,000, 40M ≈ $1.9M) — confirm the rate and the rounding convention with the operator. And confirm whether 14M is committed/pledged or received; the label must say which, because a US donor will read "committed" and "raised" very differently.

Do not

  • Do not turn this into a thermometer, a bulb, a stack of bricks that fill in, or an animated counter that ticks up on scroll. The congregation gave sacrificially; the graphic should be quiet about it.
  • Do not animate the fill on load beyond the single inline-size transition, and let prefers-reduced-motion kill it.
  • Do not add a deadline, a countdown, or "only X days left". There is no deadline on this project.
  • Do not put the percentage in a large gold figure. Percentages go in ink; gold is the fill only.
  • Do not use the perforation texture anywhere else on the same page except one hero band — it is this component's signature.
