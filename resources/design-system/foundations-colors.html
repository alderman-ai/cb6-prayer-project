Foundations

Colour

Four confirmed brand colours, plus derived families that come out of the building itself: the pale oak of the seating, the grey ribbed wall panels, the terrazzo floor. Ink carries every word. Gold is an accent and a fill — never a word on paper.

Approved pairings

Ink panel

Footer, trust block, the one dark moment on the page. Gold reads beautifully here — this is where gold is allowed to be a word.

Gold fill

The primary CTA and nothing else. If gold appears as a filled block twice on a page, one of them is wrong.

Cream ground

Verse blocks, giving details, the progress section. Warmth without weight.

Stone ground

Image placeholders, timeline, quiet structural blocks. Cooler and more factual than cream.

Textures

Two motifs, taken directly off the building. Each appears at most once or twice per artefact.

Perforation, warm

--pattern-perforation · The punched white stone screen on the new entrance. Signature motif. Reserved for the unfunded remainder of the progress bar and one hero band.

Perforation, stone

--pattern-perforation-stone · Cooler variant for use over stone grounds or where the warm dots read as noise.

Ribbing

--pattern-ribbing · The grey vertical wall panelling inside the sanctuary. Section dividers and the thin vertical rhythm above a section header.

#E08A62 — reference only, not a UI colour. This is the existing house's terracotta façade as it appears in the renders. It is documented so that imagery and interface do not fight each other. Never use it as a fill, a rule, or text.

Do not

  • Do not use #E76F51 or any "cred" value. It was inferred from a malformed five-digit hex in the site CSS and has never been confirmed by the client.
  • Do not set gold text on paper at any size. 1.7:1. Put the gold behind ink instead.
  • Do not use gold as a second fill colour on the same page as the CTA. One gold block, one meaning: give.
  • Do not introduce a red, a green, or a success/error hue. Nothing on this page reports a system state.
  • Do not use #1E87F0 from logo.svg — that asset is legacy and unreferenced on the live site.
