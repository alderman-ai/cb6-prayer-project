Components

Cards

Three card types and nothing else: the giving-details card that carries the money, the info card that carries a fact, and the verse block. All of them are flat, hairline-bordered and quiet — a shadow appears only when a card is a link and the pointer is on it.

Info cards

The scan layer. One fact per card, a heading you could read alone and still understand. Cards that link get the gold-deep border on hover; cards that do not, stay flat.

Verse block

Italic Raleway, one ribbed rule on the leading edge. One verse per artefact. It sits before the ask, never as the ask.

But who am I, and who are my people, that we should be able to give as generously as this? Everything comes from you, and we have given you only what comes from your hand. 1 Chronicles 29:14

Vždyť co jsem já a co je můj lid, že máme možnost takto dobrovolně dávat? Od tebe pochází všechno, a co jsme ti dali, pochází z tvé ruky. 1. Paralipomenon 29:14

On ink, for the closing panel. The rule picks up gold; the citation becomes the only gold text on the page, and it is legible because the ground is dark. On-ink variant

Do not

  • Do not put a drop shadow on a card at rest. Shadow signals "this is liftable" — only linked cards lift, and only on hover.
  • Do not paraphrase the bank details or reformat the variable symbol. A wrong digit is a lost gift.
  • Do not hot-link the Paylibo QR image inside the PDF. Download it once and embed it.
  • Do not stack more than one verse per artefact, and never use a verse as a headline or a button label.
  • Do not add an icon set. The arch mark is the only pictogram in this system, because it is the actual door of the building.
