Buttons

One gold button per screen, and gold is used for nothing else. Everything beside it is ink outline or a quiet link. A pill radius keeps the CTA from reading as a software product; the gold-deep border means it still holds its shape in a black-and-white print of the brochure.

Do not

  • Do not put more than three in-page buttons on the landing page (hero, giving tiers, after the trust block), plus the persistent header button. More reads as pressure.
  • Do not write "Donate now", "Act fast", or anything with a deadline attached. There is no countdown on this project.
  • Do not remove the --color-gold-deep border on the primary. It is what keeps the button visible on a white page in greyscale print.
  • Do not use gold for a secondary or ghost button. Gold means one thing on this page.
  • Do not animate the button beyond the colour transition and the 1px press. No pulse, no glow, no scale.
