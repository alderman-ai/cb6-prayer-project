Foundations

Type

Raleway, and only Raleway. It is what the church already publishes in, it is SIL-licensed so it embeds in a print PDF without a fee, and its latin-ext subset carries every Czech diacritic. The personality comes from how it is set, not from a second face: tight tracking on the large sizes, and one wide-tracked uppercase eyebrow per block — the single, licence-free nod to the Novecento Wide wordmark in the logo.

Weights

Four romans and four italics are loaded. In practice: 400 for body, 600 for every heading and the eyebrow, 700 for figures only. 500 is a fallback for dense UI labels. Italic is for scripture and captions, never for emphasis inside a sentence.

Czech & diacritics

The Czech build must load the latin-ext subset. Without it, ě š č ř ž ů fall back to a system face mid-word and the whole page loses its voice.

Raleway's caron and ring accents sit high and narrow. At Display size, tracking of −0.022em keeps them from touching the ascenders on the line above — check any Czech headline that runs to two lines, and add line-height: 1.12 locally if the accents collide.

Figures

All money uses font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums so CZK and USD columns align. Czech convention groups with a thin space (54 000 000 Kč); English convention uses commas. Set each language build in its own convention rather than picking one for both.

In situ

How the eyebrow, heading and lead sit together. The arch mark is the building's own front door, drawn at 12px; it replaces a bullet or a numbered marker, neither of which would be true here.

Where the project stands

Fourteen million koruna, given by a congregation of a few hundred.

The members have already committed 14M CZK of their own — roughly a quarter of the total, given sacrificially over the past two years. What remains is 40M.

Do not

  • Do not add a second typeface. Raleway carries display, body and figures; a serif display would read as a different institution than cb6.cz.
  • Do not re-set the logo wordmark in Raleway. The logo is Novecento Wide DemiBold, a commercial face — use logo-var1.png as supplied, or request an outlined vector.
  • Do not set body copy above 64 characters per line, or a hero sub above 52.
  • Do not use uppercase for anything except the eyebrow. Uppercase headlines read as shouting, and Czech diacritics suffer badly in caps.
  • Do not use bold (700) for emphasis in running text. Use semibold on the heading above it, or restructure the sentence.
