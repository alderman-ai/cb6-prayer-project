Files live in ../Modlitebna Images/. Every image on the page is of this building — no stock photography of generic worship, in any block.

  • 12_sanctuary-from-main-entrance.jpg — primary hero. 1920×1080. Pale oak seating, ribbed grey walls, vaulted ceiling, black cross under a skylight.
  • 16_sanctuary-from-last-row.jpg — secondary hero / alternate. Seated eye-level view; warmer and more human, better for the email header.
  • 02_proposed-design-overlay-on-photo.jpg — the street-level "after". 3949×2954, print-safe. Pairs with 01 for the before/after block.
  • 11_section-drawing-sanctuary-hall.jpg — the cutaway section. The only image that proves the hall is underground, which is the whole cost argument.
  • 03_exterior-street-view-render.jpg — the welcoming public frontage. 960×540 only, screen use.

Print ceiling: the 2025 renders top out at 1920px, which is about 160mm wide at 300dpi. A full-bleed render on A4 or US Letter needs the architect's originals — request them from modlitebna@cb6.cz before laying out the brochure.

Do not

  • Do not overlay the headline on the rendering. The renders are pale and low-contrast; text on them either disappears or forces a scrim that ruins the light. Keep type beside the image, not on it.
  • Do not apply the arch radius to more than one image per page. Everywhere else, images get --radius-md.
  • Do not use the perforation band more than once per artefact — it belongs to the progress bar.
  • Do not place logo-var1.png or logo-var1.svg directly on paper. Both are white-on-transparent and will vanish. Use the ink plate, or request a light-ground logo from the client.
  • Do not add a scroll indicator, a parallax layer, or an entrance animation on the hero image.
