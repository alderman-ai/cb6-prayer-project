Section header — left aligned, on cream

Why it costs what it costs

Most of the hall has to be dug out beneath the existing house.

Prague regulations cap how high the building on Evropská can go, so the sanctuary is excavated below ground and tucked under the house that is already there. Excavation, not finishes, is what makes a 54M Kč project out of a room with 200 chairs in it.

Do not

  • Do not run two ink sections on one page. The footer is the dark moment; a second one flattens it.
  • Do not crop the before and after differently, or shrink one of them. The pair only works if the frames are identical.
  • Do not renumber the timeline as 01 / 02 / 03. The dates are the argument.
  • Do not let the timeline go stale — re-check cb6.cz/modlitebna before each publication and update or remove the "in progress" marker.
  • Do not add social icons, a newsletter box, or a sitemap to the footer. Three contacts and one link is the whole footer.
  • Do not use the ribbing mark more than once per section header. It is punctuation, not a border.
