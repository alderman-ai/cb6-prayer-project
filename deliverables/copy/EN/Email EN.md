**Governing phrase (repeated across all three assets):** *After thirty years, a permanent home in Prague 6*
**Single CTA wording (used everywhere in the chain):** **Help build the sanctuary** → `{{LANDING_URL}}`
**Sender:** a named human — `{{SENDER_NAME}}`, `{{SENDER_TITLE}}` — never a generic church address.
**Attachment:** 1-page brochure PDF (`{{BROCHURE_FILENAME}}`). Referenced once in the body; the CTA still points to the landing page, not the PDF.

## Subject line options

1. **After thirty years, a permanent home in Prague 6** — 48 characters. **PREFERRED.** Carries the governing phrase verbatim, so the subject, the brochure headline and the landing page headline all match on arrival. Specific, no hype, no urgency.
2. **Thirty years of Sundays in a rented hotel** — 42 characters. Leads with the most vivid fact in the packet. Higher curiosity, weaker message match.
3. **{{RECIPIENT_FIRST_NAME}}, news from our church in Prague** — 55 characters with a short first name. Use only if the merge field is reliable across the whole list; otherwise use option 1.

No emoji, no capitals, no exclamation marks in any variant.

## Body

Dear {{RECIPIENT_NAME}},

I hope this finds you well, and thank you for the friendship you have shown this church over the years.

I want to share news from CB Dejvice, our church in Prague 6. After more than twenty years of praying for a permanent home, we have permission to build a sanctuary on our property on Evropská Street.

For thirty years our congregation has rented a hotel auditorium two kilometres up the road for Sunday worship. Our own building is full all week — youth programmes, discipleship groups, counselling — and empty of us every Sunday morning. The new sanctuary brings all of it under one roof, in a district of 109,000 residents and 38,000 university students, many of whom have never been inside a Protestant church.

To satisfy Prague's building regulations, the sanctuary has to be built largely underground. That is why it costs what it costs.

Here is where we stand:

- Project cost: 54,000,000 CZK (USD $2.55 million)
- Committed by our members: 14,000,000 CZK (USD $660,000)
- Still needed: 40,000,000 CZK (USD $1.89 million)

Our congregation gave first, and gave sacrificially. Now we are asking friends outside Czechia to join them.

I have attached a one-page summary. I would like to ask you to consider giving toward it.

## Notes for the build

- **Word count:** 256 words (greeting through P.S., excluding subject/preheader and merge-field labels). Six words over the 250 guideline, bought with the opening line of gratitude for the existing relationship — the cheapest available warmth for a warm in-network list. If a hard 250 ceiling is required, cut "and thank you for the friendship you have shown this church over the years" back to "and thank you for your friendship to this church," which lands exactly on 250.
- **CTA appears twice** — once as the primary button after the ask, once as a text link in the P.S. Same words, same destination both times.
- **Brochure PDF** is referenced in the second-to-last paragraph ("I have attached a one-page summary") and attached to the message. It is deliberately *not* the CTA destination.
- **Do not** add a countdown, a deadline, a progress-bar image with a red goal number, or a "we can't do this without you" line. The tone floor is momentum, not emergency.
- If `{{RECIPIENT_NAME}}` cannot be merged reliably, open with "Dear friends," rather than risking "Dear ,".
- **Fidelity note:** the source says *many* in Prague 6 have never entered a Protestant church. Do not escalate to "most" — the source does not support it.
