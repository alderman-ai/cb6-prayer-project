## Block 3 — The story (~120 words)

**Section heading:** Where we are now

CB Dejvice was founded in 1995, just after the fall of communism, to bring the hope of the Gospel to Prague 6. Thirty years on we are about 150 members — large by Czech standards — in a district of 109,000 residents and 38,000 university students, many of whom have never been inside a Protestant church.

In 2001 we bought a building on Evropská Street, the main road through Prague 6, and have spent years renovating it. Today it is full every day of the week with youth programmes, discipleship groups, counselling and community events. Every room is in use.

And every Sunday the whole congregation leaves it and walks two kilometres to a rented communist-era hotel auditorium to worship.

## Block 5 — Why it costs what it costs

**Section heading:** Built underground

To meet Prague's building regulations, the sanctuary must be constructed primarily below ground, tucked beneath the existing house. That is significantly more expensive than a conventional addition. It is the price of building a church on this street, and we believe the constraint is part of God's provision rather than an obstacle to it.

**Image:** `11_section-drawing-sanctuary-hall.jpg` — the cutaway section showing the hall sitting below ground level. This drawing answers the cost objection faster than any sentence can. Place it directly beside this block.

## Notes for the build

- **Palette:** ink `#264653`, primary accent `#E9C46A`, light accent `#FFD56E`, paper white. Reserve the gold for the CTA and the financial rule and nothing else.
- **Type:** Raleway (SIL OFL — free to embed in PDF). Latin subset is sufficient for the English edition.
- **Not included, deliberately:** donor-recognition tiers, naming rights, a giving ladder, and any deadline. Each is a client call, not a copy decision — flag them rather than assuming them.
- **Also deliberately absent:** any claim that a US-deductible giving route exists or is in progress. None has been established. If the client confirms that a named US 501(c)(3) has agreed to receive and grant the funds, the tax disclosure in Block 8 needs a rewrite and the partner's counsel should approve the wording.
- **Fidelity note:** the source says *many* in Prague 6 have never entered a Protestant church, not "most." Do not escalate.
- **Image permissions** for the renders have not been documented in writing. Confirm with modlitebna@cb6.cz before publication.
- **Print resolution:** the 2025 renders cap at 1920 px, which is about 160 mm wide at 300 dpi. If any image runs full-bleed, request the architect's originals.
