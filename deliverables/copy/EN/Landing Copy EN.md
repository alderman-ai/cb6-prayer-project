## Block 2 — The story

**Section heading:** A church without a room of its own

CB Dejvice was founded in 1995, just after the fall of communism, to bring the hope of the Gospel to Prague 6. Thirty years on, we are a congregation of about 150 members — large by Czech standards — in a district of 109,000 residents and 38,000 university students, many of whom have never set foot in a Protestant church. God has already used this church to plant another congregation in Prague.

In 2001 we bought a building on Evropská Street with a long-term hope: that one day it could hold everything we do. We have spent the years since renovating it. It is now full every day of the week with youth programmes, discipleship groups, counselling, community events and outreach. Every room is in use.

## Block 3 — Before and after

Then Sunday comes, and the entire congregation walks two kilometres up the road to a rented communist-era hotel auditorium. We have done this for more than thirty years. ## Block 4 — Why it costs this much

**Section heading:** Built underground

Prague 6's building regulations will not permit a conventional extension on this site. To build at all, the sanctuary has to go primarily below ground, excavated and constructed beneath the existing house. That is far more expensive than adding a hall on the side, and it is the single reason a 150-member church is looking at a 54-million-crown project.

We are not building something grand. We are paying what this city charges for a room to worship in.

## Block 8 — FAQ

**Section heading:** Questions people ask

**Is my gift tax-deductible in the United States?**
No. A US charitable deduction is only available for gifts to organisations formed in the United States, and CB Dejvice is a Czech church. A gift sent directly to us is not deductible on a US return, and UK donors cannot claim Gift Aid. If this matters to your giving, write to hospodar@cb6.cz before you send anything.

**What currency am I charged in, and how does the money reach you?**
Gifts arrive as an international bank transfer in Czech crowns (CZK) to the church's sanctuary account, carrying the payment reference shown in the giving section above. Your own bank sets the exchange rate and any transfer fee. Full account details are in the giving section.

**Who receives the money, and what happens if the project changes?**
Gifts go into the church's dedicated sanctuary account at Raiffeisenbank, held by CB Dejvice and designated for the sanctuary project by the payment reference. Project updates, drawings and permit progress are published at [cb6.cz/modlitebna](https://www.cb6.cz/modlitebna). Questions about how funds are handled go to hospodar@cb6.cz.

**Can our church or foundation give as an institution?**
Yes. Partner churches, foundations and institutional donors are welcome, and we are glad to answer diligence questions directly — hospodar@cb6.cz for the financial side, modlitebna@cb6.cz for the project.

**Why is a sanctuary for 150 people so expensive?**
Because Prague 6's regulations require it to be built primarily underground, beneath the existing building. Excavation and underground construction cost several times what a conventional hall would. See the section drawing above.

**Why not keep renting?**
The hotel auditorium costs real money every week, sits two kilometres from the building where the rest of our life happens, and is invisible to the neighbourhood we are trying to reach. Building ends a recurring cost and puts the church where people can find it.

**Can I see the actual plans?**
Yes. Photographs, floor plans, section drawings and the full gallery of renderings are published at [cb6.cz/modlitebna](https://www.cb6.cz/modlitebna). Project questions go to modlitebna@cb6.cz.

## Notes for the build

- **Body word count:** roughly 900 words of reader-facing copy across blocks 2–8, excluding headings and the image/display notes. At the top of the 600–900 target; the additions are the two institutional FAQ entries.
- **Three CTA buttons only** — hero, after the underground block, after the giving details — plus the sticky header button. Identical wording each time, all anchoring to Block 7. Do not add more; more reads as pressure.
- **Bank name unverified.** "Raiffeisenbank" (Block 7 and the "Who receives the money" FAQ) is inferred from bank code 5500 in the published account 1031051032/5500. Confirm with the client before publishing, or drop the bank name.
- **Deliberately absent:** giving tiers, suggested amounts, a named leadership-gift level, a monthly/pledge option, and any "other ways to give" grid (stock, DAF, cheque). All are recommended by the research and all are client calls — flag them, do not assume them. If the client adds suggested amounts, they belong in Block 7 above the bank details.
- **Also deliberately absent:** any claim that a US-deductible route exists or is in progress. None has been established. If the client confirms that a named US 501(c)(3) has agreed to receive and grant the funds, Blocks 1, 7 and 8 all need a rewrite and the partner's counsel should approve the wording.
- **Also deliberately absent:** any statement that the sanctuary account is segregated from the church's operating account, or that the members' 14 million crowns is held in it. Both would be strong trust copy, and neither is supported by anything the client has put in writing. Ask; do not assert.
- **Committed ≠ paid.** Nothing on this page may describe the members' 14 million crowns as paid, received, banked or raised.
- **Mobile:** consider reordering so Block 5 (the figures) precedes Block 2, giving a phone visitor the concrete ask before the narrative. Single-column stack, full-width tap targets, no horizontal scroll. Cookie consent as a slim top bar, never a bottom sheet over the CTA.
- **Imagery:** church and architect images only, no stock photography, from `Resources/Modlitebna Images/`. Compress for sub-3-second mobile load. Image reuse permission is undocumented — confirm with modlitebna@cb6.cz before publishing.
