CB Dejvice · Církev bratrská · Prague 6, Czech Republic

After thirty years, a permanent home in Prague 6

A healthy and growing church, building a sanctuary for its neighbours.

Where we are now

CB Dejvice was founded in 1995, just after the fall of communism, to bring the hope of the Gospel to Prague 6. Thirty years on we are about 150 members — large by Czech standards — in a district of 109,000 residents and 38,000 university students, many of whom have never been inside a Protestant church.

In 2001 we bought a building on Evropská Street, the main road through Prague 6, and have spent years renovating it. Today it is full every day of the week with youth programmes, discipleship groups, counselling and community events. Every room is in use.

And every Sunday the whole congregation leaves it and walks two kilometres to a rented communist-era hotel auditorium to worship.

What the sanctuary makes possible

  • A permanent, welcoming place for worship, discipleship and outreach — all of it under one roof.
  • Better service to families, students and the neighbourhood around us.
  • A visible, inviting public presence, so our neighbours can see that a church is here and that they are welcome inside.
  • Room to grow, including multiple Sunday services as new people explore faith.
  • An end to long-term dependence on expensive rented facilities.

Built underground

To meet Prague's building regulations, the sanctuary must be constructed primarily below ground, tucked beneath the existing house. That is significantly more expensive than a conventional addition. It is the price of building a church on this street, and we believe the constraint is part of God's provision rather than an obstacle to it.
